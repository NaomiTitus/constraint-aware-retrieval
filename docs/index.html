Smart Search

Job search over 10,166 Norwegian advertisements that respects what you cannot do, not just what you want.

λ 0.7 — how hard constraints bite

Try: nurse, Bergen, no Norwegian · data scientist, English only · tømrer (Norwegian) · teacher, no Norwegian · engineer in Trondheim

    How the ranking works. Your text is parsed into typed attributes — an occupation resolved against ESCO's bilingual labels, a language capability, and free-text skills. A job's requirements R are viable only if contained in your attributes S. Occupation is compared as a STYRK taxonomy code (2223 nurse vs 2221 specialist nurse share a group), so nurse and sykepleier reach the same ads. Language is a graded penalty × λ, never a filter — at λ=0 the constraint stage is off, and you can watch the ranking change.

    Honest limitations. Skills come from the original census and are present on only 32.8% of ads; a re-prompt measured 8× better recall but has not been run over the corpus. Location is a graded predicate over the corpus's own municipality and county names (exact 1.0, same county 0.55), and every location an ad lists is checked. No relevance judgments have been applied to this ranking yet, so nothing here is a measured quality claim. Ad text, employer names and contact details are deliberately not redistributed — each result links to the original advertisement on arbeidsplassen.no. Corpus snapshot 2026-09-24; many ads will have expired.
