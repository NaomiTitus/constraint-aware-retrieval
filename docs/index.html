Smart Search

Job search over 10,166 Norwegian advertisements that respects what you cannot do, not just what you want.

λ 0.7 — how hard constraints bite

Try: nurse, Bergen, no Norwegian · data scientist, English only · tømrer (Norwegian) · teacher, no Norwegian · engineer in Trondheim

    How the ranking works. Your text is parsed into typed attributes — an occupation resolved against ESCO's bilingual labels, a language capability, and free-text skills. A job's requirements R are viable only if contained in your attributes S. Occupation is compared as a STYRK taxonomy code (2223 nurse vs 2221 specialist nurse share a group), so nurse and sykepleier reach the same ads. Language works two ways: a graded penalty × λ on advertisements that demand what you lack, and a BOOST for the 956 the census marks open to English speakers. Never a filter — at λ=0 the constraint stage is off, and you can watch the ranking change.

    What it does with language. If you say you do not speak Norwegian, advertisements demanding it are demoted by a graded severity (a stated requirement counts more than silence) and advertisements explicitly open to English are promoted. If you say nothing, the stage contributes exactly nothing — writing in English is observed and reported, never silently treated as a constraint, because a third of this corpus is merely silent on language and inferring a requirement from silence would remove 3,507 jobs from everyone.

    Honest limitations. Skills come from the original census and are present on only 32.8% of ads; a re-prompt measured 8× better recall but has not been run over the corpus. Location is a graded predicate over the corpus's own municipality and county names (exact 1.0, same county 0.55), and every location an ad lists is checked. No relevance judgments have been applied to this ranking yet, so nothing here is a measured quality claim. Ad text, employer names and contact details are deliberately not redistributed — each result links to the original advertisement on arbeidsplassen.no. Corpus snapshot 2026-09-24; many ads will have expired.
